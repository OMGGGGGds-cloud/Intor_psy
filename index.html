📘 Theory 1: 人際自殺理論 (IPT) DOI: 10.1037/a0018697

受挫歸屬感（我覺得沒人愛我）＋知覺累贅感（沒有我大家更開心）產生自殺意念；加上長期體罰導致對痛楚麻木（習得性能力），三者交會引發悲劇（Van Orden et al., 2010; Joiner et al., 2009）。

📗 Theory 2: 習得性無助與絕望理論 DOI: 10.1037/0033-295X.96.2.358

無論多努力背書都被打罵，使有傑形成內在、穩定、全面的悲觀歸因（「是我太蠢、永遠不會變好」），最終發展為對未來的徹底絕望（Seligman, 1972; Abramson et al., 1978, 1989; Beck et al., 1985）。

📊 香港學童數據與媒體效應 DOI: 10.1521/suli.2006.36.1.82

香港研究顯示 47%–52.6% 中學生曾有自殺念頭（Lee et al., 2006），7.3% 曾蓄意自殘（Wong et al., 2005）。以正向求助故事（Papageno 效應）取代模仿效應（Werther 效應）能有效挽救生命（Niederkrotenthaler et al., 2010）。

PSY2101 心理學理論與 APA 7th 文獻對照表（附 DOI）

1. 人際自殺理論 (Interpersonal Theory of Suicide) & 三階段理論 (3ST)

解釋為何有傑從「痛苦」走向「行動」：當受挫歸屬感 (Thwarted Belongingness) 與知覺累贅感 (Perceived Burdensomeness) 同時出現且對未來絕望，便會產生強烈自殺意念；而長期遭受父親體罰則降低了對痛楚與死亡的恐懼（習得性自殺能力 Acquired Capability）。相反地，只要有一位關懷的傾聽者恢復其歸屬感，就能阻斷自殺意念（Klonsky & May, 2015）。

  • Van Orden, K. A., et al. (2010). The interpersonal theory of suicide. Psychological Review, 117(2), 575–600. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018697
  • Joiner, T. E., Jr., et al. (2009). Main predictions of the interpersonal–psychological theory of suicidal behavior. Journal of Abnormal Psychology, 118(3), 634–646. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016500
  • Chu, C., et al. (2017). The interpersonal theory of suicide: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Psychological Bulletin, 143(12), 1313–1345. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000123
  • Klonsky, E. D., & May, A. M. (2015). The Three-Step Theory (3ST). International Journal of Cognitive Therapy, 8(2), 114–129. https://doi.org/10.1521/ijct.2015.8.2.114

2. 習得性無助 (Learned Helplessness) 與絕望抑鬱理論 (Hopelessness Theory)

有傑無論多努力溫習、對河馬公仔喊「再努力一點」，仍換來留級與毒打。這種「努力與結果完全脫鉤」的不可控懲罰，導致他產生內在 (Internal)、穩定 (Stable)、全面 (Global) 的悲觀歸因風格，最終陷入絕望感 (Hopelessness)。

  • Seligman, M. E. P. (1972). Learned helplessness. Annual Review of Medicine, 23(1), 407–412. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.me.23.020172.002203
  • Abramson, L. Y., Seligman, M. E. P., & Teasdale, J. D. (1978). Learned helplessness in humans: Critique and reformulation. Journal of Abnormal Psychology, 87(1), 49–74. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-843X.87.1.49
  • Abramson, L. Y., Metalsky, G. I., & Alloy, L. B. (1989). Hopelessness depression: A theory-based subtype of depression. Psychological Review, 96(2), 358–372. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.96.2.358
  • Beck, A. T., et al. (1985). Hopelessness and eventual suicide: A 10-year prospective study. American Journal of Psychiatry, 142(5), 559–563. https://doi.org/10.1176/ajp.142.5.559

3. 香港學生自殺數據與媒體傳染／保護效應 (Werther vs. Papageno Effects)

香港本地研究指出近半中學生曾出現自殺念頭（47.0%–52.6%），考試焦慮（r = .51）、低家庭支持（r = -.48）及父母不滿（r = .35）為核心預測因子；同時媒體對名人或虛構角色自殺的渲染會引發模仿（維特效應 Werther Effect），而呈現正面求助與渡過危機的報導則具保護作用（帕帕基諾效應 Papageno Effect）。

  • Lee, M. T. Y., Wong, B. P., Chow, B. W.-Y., & McBride-Chang, C. (2006). Predictors of suicide ideation and depression in Hong Kong adolescents. Suicide and Life-Threatening Behavior, 36(1), 82–96. https://doi.org/10.1521/suli.2006.36.1.82
  • Wong, J. P. S., et al. (2005). Exposure to suicide and suicidal behaviors among Hong Kong adolescents. Social Science & Medicine, 61(3), 591–599. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2004.12.012
  • Niederkrotenthaler, T., et al. (2010). Role of media reports in completed and prevented suicide: Werther v. Papageno effects. British Journal of Psychiatry, 197(3), 234–243. https://doi.org/10.1192/bjp.bp.109.074633
  • Yip, P. S. F., et al. (2006). The effects of a celebrity suicide on suicide rates in Hong Kong. Journal of Affective Disorders, 93(1–3), 245–252. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2006.03.015
  • Bridge, J. A., et al. (2020). Association between the release of Netflix's 13 Reasons Why and suicide rates in the United States. JAACAP, 59(2), 236–243. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaac.2019.04.020
